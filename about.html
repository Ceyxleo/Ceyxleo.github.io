About

Lingbo Li, Ph.D.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering and the Center for Catastrophe Modeling and Resilience at Lehigh University, working with Prof. Hong-Yi Li. I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the University of Houston in December 2025.

My research asks how water, carbon and human management interact across river networks. I build that understanding into Earth system models, mainly the land and river components of E3SM, and use observations and machine learning to constrain them: national and global maps that parameterize river carbon, emulators that stand in for expensive physics, and AI decision policies coupled into simulations.

I care about work others can trust and reuse, so I check conservation and test against earlier runs, and I release data and code with DOIs.
