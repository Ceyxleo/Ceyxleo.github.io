E3SM · 2026–

MOSART-Lake: lakes in a global river model

I develop lake water balance, outflow, temperature, and snow and ice for 49,708 lake objects in MOSART-Lake, the river component of the DOE Energy Exascale Earth System Model (E3SM). For lake ice I built an LSTM emulator with a locked temporal test period, physically constrained outputs, and numerical parity checks between the training code and the deployed version. I evaluate multi-decade simulations against satellite lake-area and surface-temperature products.

  • Fortran
  • E3SM / MOSART
  • LSTM emulator
  • NetCDF
  • Water & heat conservation
My role & scope

Model development, the snow and ice emulator, simulation diagnostics and the manuscript draft. The model is still under development, so performance numbers will be shared after publication.

Ph.D. · JAMES 2026 · ESSD 2025

Land-to-river dissolved organic carbon

My doctoral work built a land-to-river carbon chain. First, I used XGBoost with river chemistry, soil-carbon and watershed data to map the DOC transformation rate for more than 2.6 million U.S. catchments (ESSD 2025). These maps parameterize ELM-MOSART-DOC, a riverine DOC model I developed in Fortran within E3SM that represents soil leaching, river transport and transformation, and reservoir regulation (JAMES 2026).

Against long-term monitoring, the model reached KGE 0.58 at large-river stations and 0.76 at local stations, and it estimates a 7.5% reservoir-associated reduction in Mississippi River DOC export to the ocean.

Global riverbed grain size for 8.5 million reaches

A global map of riverbed median grain size (D50) for about 8.5 million HydroRIVERS reaches. We harmonized sieve and literature observations, matched them to the river network with data-quality controls, trained XGBoost models, and mapped where the predictions are applicable.

A riverbed particle-size distribution with physical meaning

A Generalized Inverse Gaussian (GIG) representation of riverbed particle-size distributions whose parameters can be read hydraulically, fitted to 19,765 U.S. samples from 2,114 stations and checked on independent Lower Rhine samples. The Python workflow compares 25 candidate distributions with multi-start fitting and BIC/AIC selection, with 47 automated tests and scripts that regenerate every table and figure.

  • Python · SciPy
  • Distribution fitting
  • Automated tests
My role & scope

Second author on Kseniia A. Gerasimova's study: distribution fitting, model comparison, analysis reproduction and the software and data release.

Global lake parameters for river models

A Python/NetCDF pipeline that links about 1.42 million HydroLAKES records and GLOBathy bathymetry to MOSART river grids at any resolution. It classifies main-channel and tributary lakes, resolves their placement from drainage area and shorelines, fits area–depth–volume curves in parallel, and writes QA/QC reports on completeness and placement.

Interpretable machine learning for beach water quality

I compared classification trees, random forest, CatBoost, XGBoost and LightGBM for E. coli-based beach water-quality prediction at three Lake Erie beaches, and used global and local SHAP explanations to connect predictions with the environmental conditions behind them. This is my most-cited paper.
